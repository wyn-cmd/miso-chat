Profile card: what users see on the home page

Relationship speed

How quickly this character's trust and affection grow, from 1.00 to 6.00 in steps of 0.01. 2.00 is the normal rate, 1.00 is half and 6.00 is five times. The defaults are 3.00 for trust and 2.00 for affection. Unkindness and trust decay are not sped up. Users see only the category, never the number.

The card is the character's persona. Keep the Colour: line as it is; the colour above updates it.

Example messages

A few short exchanges that show how this character speaks: the shape of its sentences, its nicknames, its habits. They are reference only. The model is told not to repeat them, and they never appear in the chat.

Separate from the card. Use it to steer pacing or tone, for example "Keep replies short and tense" or "Let the scene breathe; do not rush the confession", without rewriting the character.

PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF, up to 10 MB. A still picture is cropped to a square and reduced to under 512 KB. An animated GIF keeps its animation, so it is stored as uploaded. Without one, a placeholder initial is shown.

Each background is shown behind this character's chat, one picked at random each time it opens, and its home page card cycles through them. Add up to 10. A still picture can be PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF up to 10 MB. An animated GIF, WebP or APNG keeps moving and can be up to 100 MB; it is stored as uploaded. A video can be MP4 or MOV up to 50 MB and at least 720p, up to 60 seconds: it is converted to WebM and MP4 with the sound removed, loops silently, and shows its first frame when the reader has asked for reduced motion.

Theme music plays while someone chats with this character or reads its card. Add up to three tracks; they play in order and start again at the top, and each one fades in and out. An MP3, AAC (M4A), Opus, WebM, Ogg or WAV file up to 60 MB and 15 minutes. A file already in a browser format at 160 kbps or lower is kept as it is. Anything else is converted to Opus at 160 kbps, with an AAC copy at 192 kbps for Safari.

Mind Palace (database searching)

Each entry joins the prompt when its keys appear in the recent chat. Keys match whole words, ignoring case and accents. Plurals and common endings (cat, cats, loved, stopping, cities) match on their own. For an irregular form, write both in one key, such as mouse|mice. Secondary keys narrow it further (see Logic). Always on entries join every reply.

                  Automatic entries

                  Each check suggests up to three entries and keeps the one whose keys come up most in the last 30 messages.                   Mind Palace check

                  An entry is orphaned when nothing has used it in the last 200 messages and for 14 days, unless you mark it as core memory. Over-firing means its keys come up in at least half of recent messages, so it takes room in the prompt each time.
