New character

Make a character one step at a time. Nothing is saved until the last step.

    How do you want to start?

    Pick a way to begin. A file fills in the steps for you to check, and you can change anything before you create the character.

    Text is read directly, and pictures and scanned pages go through OCR. Pictures are never used as the profile or background picture.

    A card fills the steps for you to check. Its picture and lorebook are kept until you create the character.

    A .zip exported from Miso Chat is created straight away, with its history if it has one. The steps are not used for it.

    Name and colour

    The name is how the character appears in the list and in chats. It is also the character's folder name, so it cannot be changed later. The colour is used for the placeholder picture and the chat bubble.

    Write the character

    The card is the character's persona: who they are, how they speak, what they want. The model reads it on every reply. Keep a Colour: line if the card has one; the colour on the name step updates it.

    Profile card

    This is what people see on the home page: a headline, a short summary and a few tags so the character is easy to find. It is never sent to the model. Auto-fill drafts all three from your card.

    Example messages

    Short exchanges that show how the character talks: the shape of its sentences, its nicknames, its habits. They are reference only and never appear in the chat. Optional, up to 12.

    Context and author's note

    Context holds the memories, places and situations the character knows. The author's note is a short instruction sent near the end of every prompt, such as pacing or tone, without rewriting the character. Both are optional.

    Relationship speed

    How quickly trust and affection grow, from 1.00 to 6.00. 2.00 is the normal rate, 1.00 is half and 6.00 is five times. The defaults are 3.00 for trust and 2.00 for affection. Users see only the category, never the number. If unsure, keep the defaults.

    Picture and backgrounds

    The picture shows beside the character in the list. Backgrounds show behind its chat, one picked at random each time it opens. Both are optional and are saved when you create the character.

    PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF, up to 10 MB. A still picture is cropped to a square and reduced to under 512 KB. Without one, a placeholder initial is shown.

    Up to 10. A still picture can be PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF up to 10 MB. An animated GIF, WebP or APNG keeps moving and can be up to 100 MB. A video can be MP4 or MOV up to 50 MB, at least 720p and up to 60 seconds; it is converted to a silent loop.

    Check and create

    Check each part. Change takes you back to that step. Create saves the character, and chats pick it up on their next message.
